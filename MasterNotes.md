### Description of the experimental design
This project works with Candida albicans, a commensal yeast that can cause a variety of pathologies. The goal of the proejct is to explore how gene Candida albicans gene expression differs in presence of thiamine and thiamine-deficient enviornments and whether this relates to C. albicans virulence. 

The data was constructed from a single biological replicate (B) that was exposed to thiamine and thiamine-defiicent enviornments. The data consists of the paired end fastqc sequencing files for both conditions.
